About the Diagnostic

The Executive Advantage Score

What is the Executive Advantage Score?
+
The Executive Advantage Score is a free, 14-step career readiness diagnostic built on Adrian Evans' proven framework for accessing the hidden job market. You rate yourself across 14 statements covering five stages of a successful executive career transition. Each score is weighted by importance and compiled into a total out of 200 — giving you an honest, objective picture of where you stand and where to focus first.
How long does it take to complete?
+
The diagnostic takes approximately 4 minutes to complete. There are 14 self-rating statements across 5 stages. You slide a bar from 0 (not at all true) to 10 (completely true) for each one. Your score, breakdown and key focus areas are shown immediately on screen when you finish.
Is it really free?
+
Yes — the diagnostic is completely free with no strings attached. Adrian reviews every result personally and follows up directly. There is no obligation to engage in any paid advisory programme.
What happens after I submit my results?
+
Once you complete the diagnostic and enter your details, your results are sent to your email address and Adrian is notified immediately. Adrian reviews your score and key focus areas and will typically follow up within 1–2 business days to discuss your results and whether advisory support would be useful.
Why do I need to use a personal email address?
+
Corporate email servers often block automated emails from external senders, which means your results may not reach your inbox if you use a work address. We recommend using a personal email (Gmail, Hotmail, Yahoo or similar) to ensure your results are delivered reliably.
What is a good score out of 200?
+
The scoring bands are: 0–80 (Not yet visible), 81–130 (Emerging access), 131–170 (Active access), and 171–200 (Hidden market insider). Most executives completing the diagnostic for the first time score in the 60–120 range — which is entirely normal. The value is not in the absolute score but in identifying the specific steps where you are weakest, as these represent the highest-leverage areas to address first.
